About me

I am a Computer Science student at Tanta University with a strong interest in Front-End Web Development. I enjoy turning ideas into well-structured and visually appealing websites using HTML and CSS, and I am continuously improving my JavaScript skills.

I have worked on several practice projects, including restaurant and coffee shop websites, a packaging company website, and portfolio designs. These projects have helped me strengthen my understanding of web page structure, styling, layout techniques, and user interface design.

I am currently developing my skills through the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI), following the Front-End Development track. My goal is to build accessible, responsive, and user-friendly websites while continuing to grow as a developer.
